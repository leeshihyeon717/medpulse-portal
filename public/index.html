PANHCE Editorial Policy & Safety Guidelines

PANHCE was established with a strict mandate: medical information must be authored exclusively by accredited, verified clinicians. To prevent dangerous misinformation, our platform implements strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

Approved Medical Editors

Only verified medical doctors (MD), clinical pharmacists (PharmD), and authorized clinical editors possess the credentials to publish, edit, or remove medical articles and medication guides.

Visitors & Patients

Visitors enjoy complete, unhindered access to read clinical articles, explore the pharmacy database, post questions in the comments section, and submit site ratings. Editing of core content is strictly forbidden for non-editors.

Medical Disclaimer: The content provided on PANHCE is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute individual medical diagnosis or substitute professional clinical judgment. Always consult your qualified physician regarding any medical condition.
